Mayflower, the max-coverage rung
================================

1. Every quantity at once, where all of them are computable
----------------------------------------------------------

maxcov(t) exactly, by scanning the subset lattice, alongside both optima and
both readings of the ladder. A valid lower bound has to sit at or below the
adaptive optimum. Watch which columns do.

instance      boards     K  E4 water  adaptive  non-adapt     maxcov   K*maxcov
4x3 {2}           17     1    4.9412    5.1176     7.4706     7.4706     7.4706
4x4 {2}           24     1    5.6667    6.0833     9.5833     9.5833     9.5833
4x4 {3}           16     1    5.0625    5.6250    10.8750    10.7500    10.7500
5x4 {3}           22     1    5.4091    6.2273    13.0455    12.8636    12.8636
4x4 {2,2}        224     2    7.8750    8.6696    12.4866    12.4777     9.9732
4x4 {3,2}        264     5    7.4697    8.7538    13.1098    13.1023     8.7841

  E4 sits at or below the adaptive optimum everywhere, as a bound must.
  The K*maxcov column exceeds it on 6 of 6 instances, so it is not a
  bound on anything. Dropping K does not rescue it either.

  What the maxcov column does obey is the non-adaptive optimum, which it
  sits at or below on every row. That is the correct reading: fixing one
  shot-set of size t is precisely the non-adaptive assumption, and an
  adaptive searcher has a tree of them. On 4x3 {2} and 4x4 {2} the two agree
  exactly, because there a single chain attains the maximum at every t.

  So the rung was measuring the wrong problem. The gap between the maxcov
  and adaptive columns is the adaptivity gap, which tools/m9 measures at up
  to 2.09x, and that is the factor by which the sketched bound overshoots.

2. What is left on the standard board
-------------------------------------

Two things survive section 1. The first is a measurement of how loose the
C(t,17) factor really is, which says where E4's slack lives even though
maxcov cannot replace it. The second is that maxcov turned out to be a
non-adaptive quantity, and the non-adaptive optimum is worth a number here.

  How far C(t,17) sits above an achievable c(S):
      t          C(t,17)  best c(S) found          ratio
     17                1               20           0.05
     20             1140             1816         0.6278
     25        1.082e+06        3.454e+04          31.31
     30        1.198e+08        3.819e+05          313.6
     40        8.873e+10        6.875e+06      1.291e+04
     50        9.847e+12        6.397e+07      1.539e+05
     60        3.872e+14          3.2e+08       1.21e+06
     70        7.878e+15        1.112e+09      7.084e+06
     80        1.015e+17        3.067e+09      3.309e+07
     90        9.344e+17        7.209e+09      1.296e+08

  The two cross over near t = 22. Below it C(t,17) is the smaller factor,
  since maxcov counts configurations and several ship decompositions share
  one cell set: at t = 17 a full row plus seven of the next holds the fleet
  20 ways against C(17,17) = 1. Above it C(t,17) runs away: by t = 90 the
  ratio is 1.3e+08, and that is where E4's slack lives and why it saturates
  early.

  None of that is reachable. Closing the gap would need a bound on maxcov
  from above, and section 1 shows that even a perfect one would be bounding
  the non-adaptive problem.

  The non-adaptive optimum, from explicit orders (each an upper bound):
  order                        E[T]    vs adaptive
  row-major                 88.7342               
  column-major              88.7342               
  alternating rows          90.2512               
  diagonal lattice          95.7404               

  Best fixed order found: row-major at 88.7342 shots, so the non-adaptive optimum is
  at most that. The density policy measures 44.369 on the same board, so an
  adaptive player beats every fixed order tried here by 44.3652 shots.

  Both of those are achievable numbers rather than optima, so the pair does
  not bound the adaptivity gap from below: that would need a lower bound on
  the non-adaptive optimum, and the only route to one is a bound on maxcov
  from above, which is the same wall section 1 hit. What the pair does show
  is that the gap is not small, matching the 2.09x tools/m9 measures where
  both optima are computable.

  Verdict on the rung: cut. E5 as sketched is not a lower bound on the
  adaptive optimum, and the ladder keeps E4 at 24.088 as its binding rung.
